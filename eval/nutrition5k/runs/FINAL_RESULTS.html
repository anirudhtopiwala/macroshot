MacroShot — meal-macro accuracy eval

How accurately can an LLM read calories & macros from a meal photo (and/or a typed description)? Benchmarked on Nutrition5K against the published baseline of Wang et al. 2026, n=100 dishes stratified by complexity. Lower error is better.

Metrics

MAE — Mean Absolute Error
Average |prediction − ground truth| in native units (kcal / grams). Primary, most interpretable.
RelErr — Relative Error (MAPE)
mean(|pred − truth| / truth) × 100%. Scale-free but inflated by tiny denominators (a 3 g fat miss on 1 g reads 300%).
MedPE — Median Percent Error
median(|pred − truth| / truth) × 100%. Robust counterpart to RelErr — 'half of dishes are within X%'. Best single UX number.
Avg* (AvgMAE / AvgRelErr / AvgMedPE)
Averaged across the five nutrients (calories, mass, fat, carbs, protein).
Wang et al. 2026 baseline (Gemini 2.5 Flash, n=3466): image-only AvgMAE 45.55 / RelErr 161%; image+ingredients 44.12 / 139%. Per-macro cells below read MAE / RelErr% · MedPE%.

Prompts

Baseline (our reconstruction of Wang's minimal prompt, sent as the user message; no system prompt):

Calculate the total calories (kcal), total weight (g), fat content (g), carbohydrate content (g), and protein content (g) for the food in this image. Reply with JSON only: {"calories": <kcal>, "mass_g": <g>, "fat_g": <g>, "carb_g": <g>, "protein_g": <g>}.

[Baseline + GT ingredients prepends: "Ingredients on this plate: <names>."]

MacroShot prompts are MacroShot's production prompts — see source on GitHub: photo prompt (CONVERSATIONAL_INITIAL_PROMPT) · text-only prompt (TEXT_ONLY_INITIAL_PROMPT). The 'MacroShot + user caption' option uses the photo prompt with the image and the user's caption together.

How the user captions were generated

For the caption / text-only options we simulate what a real user would type. Each caption was generated by Gemini from the dish's ground-truth ingredient list (names + grams), under strict rules: write a casual log entry, never leak exact grams or macros, stay literal (don't invent dishes or cooking methods), skip sub-1 g seasonings. Two lengths per dish — terse (~10–15 words) and detailed (~25–35 words, with vague portion cues like 'a palm-sized portion'). Captions were verified faithful to the ingredients (≈76% coverage, ~0 hallucinations).

Ground-truth ingredients (input to Gemini)
corn; garlic; caesar salad; nopales; olive oil; pepper; green beans; lime; sour cream; jicama; arugula; fish; carrot
→ Terse caption (used by 'Text-only terse' & 'MacroShot + user caption')
“Had fish with caesar salad, green beans, corn, and some other veggies.”
→ Detailed caption (used by 'Text-only detailed')
“Had a good portion of fish, a side of caesar salad, and green beans. Also a small mix of corn and other veggies, with olive oil, lime, and sour cream.”
Same caption, with vs without the photo. The identical terse caption above is fed to BOTH “MacroShot + user caption (terse)” (photo prompt + image + caption) and “Text-only (terse)” (text prompt + caption, no image). Holding the user's exact words constant, comparing those two isolates precisely what the photo adds. Likewise the detailed caption is what separates the two Text-only options.
