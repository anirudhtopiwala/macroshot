2 · Reproducing the paper

Our BASELINE on Opus 4.7 = 46.9 ≈ Wang's 45.55 ✅ (anchor). Flash-Lite 54.5 (close). Flash-full = 89 — its chain-of-thought over-estimates portions (+203 kcal bias); reasoning ≠ free accuracy. Anchor repro on Opus, not Flash-full.

3 · The caption fix (X3 → X3v2)

Modelcaption flow: before → after
Flash-Lite (shipped)83.0 → 56.6 (-32%)
Opus 4.850.8 → 41.1 (-19%)
An accurate caption made the model stack a standard serving per named item → inflation. The fix (caption=identity, image=portion, whole-plate budget) helps both models — bigger on the small one (−32%) than the frontier (−19%), and wins on Flash-full too (X3v2 67.3 vs baseline 89).
