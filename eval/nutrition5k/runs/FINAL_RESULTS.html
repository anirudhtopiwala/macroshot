MacroShot — meal-macro accuracy eval

How accurately can an LLM read calories & macros from a meal photo (and/or a typed description)? Benchmarked on Nutrition5K against the published baseline of Wang et al. 2026, n=100 dishes stratified by complexity. Lower error is better.

Metrics

MAE — Mean Absolute Error
Average |prediction − ground truth| in native units (kcal / grams). Primary, most interpretable.
RelErr — Relative Error (MAPE)
mean(|pred − truth| / truth) × 100%. Scale-free but inflated by tiny denominators (a 3 g fat miss on 1 g reads 300%).
MedPE — Median Percent Error
median(|pred − truth| / truth) × 100%. Robust counterpart to RelErr — 'half of dishes are within X%'. Best single UX number.
Avg* (AvgMAE / AvgRelErr / AvgMedPE)
Averaged across the five nutrients (calories, mass, fat, carbs, protein).
Wang et al. 2026 baseline (Gemini 2.5 Flash, n=3466): image-only AvgMAE 45.55 / RelErr 161%; image+ingredients 44.12 / 139%. Per-macro cells below read MAE / RelErr% · MedPE%.

How the user captions were generated

For the caption / text-only options we simulate what a real user would type. Each caption was generated by Gemini from the dish’s ground-truth ingredient list, under strict rules: write a casual log entry, never leak exact grams or macros, stay literal (don’t invent dishes or methods), skip sub-1 g seasonings. The generator does see each ingredient’s gram weight and uses it to shape the detailed caption’s vague portion cues ('a good portion', 'a small side') — but never states a number. So 'detailed' carries a mild, GT-derived portion hint that 'terse' (no portions) does not. Captions were verified faithful (≈76% ingredient coverage, ~0 hallucinations).

Ground-truth ingredients (input to Gemini)
corn; garlic; caesar salad; nopales; olive oil; pepper; green beans; lime; sour cream; jicama; arugula; fish; carrot
→ Terse caption
“Had fish with caesar salad, green beans, corn, and some other veggies.”
→ Detailed caption
“Had a good portion of fish, a side of caesar salad, and green beans. Also a small mix of corn and other veggies, with olive oil, lime, and sour cream.”
Same caption, with vs without the photo. The identical terse caption is fed to BOTH MacroShot + user caption (terse) (photo prompt + image + caption) and Text-only (terse) (text prompt + caption, no image). Holding the user’s exact words constant, comparing those two isolates precisely what the photo adds. The detailed caption is what separates the two Text-only options.
